Frequently Asked Questions

For recognised-sponsor filings, the Netherlands is typically the fastest route in Europe. Most India → Netherlands relocations are completed in 6–10 weeks end to end, including document preparation, IND filing, visa collection, travel, and local registration. IND decision timelines are typically around 4 weeks for recognised sponsors, though timelines vary based on case complexity and consulate appointment availability.

The salary threshold depends on the destination country and role category.

For Germany (2026):
General EU Blue Card threshold: €50,700 / year
Shortage occupations and IT roles: €45,934.20 / year

For the Netherlands:
EU Blue Card threshold: €5,942 / month
Reduced threshold categories apply in some cases.

Thresholds are revised periodically by local authorities and should always be checked against the latest immigration guidance before filing.

The kennismigrant (Highly Skilled Migrant) route is the most commonly used sponsorship route in the Netherlands and is tied directly to recognised sponsors registered with the IND.

The EU Blue Card is an EU-wide framework designed for highly qualified professionals and may offer additional long-term mobility advantages across EU member states.

In practice:

  • HSM is usually faster and operationally simpler for Dutch employers.
  • EU Blue Card may be more useful for professionals planning multi-country EU mobility later.

The correct route depends on salary level, degree recognition, employer structure, and long-term plans.

An erkend referent is a company officially recognised by the Dutch IND to sponsor foreign employees under immigration routes such as the Highly Skilled Migrant programme.

Recognised sponsors can:

  • file directly with the IND,
  • access faster processing channels,
  • sponsor employees compliantly,
  • and manage immigration responsibilities within Dutch regulatory frameworks.
